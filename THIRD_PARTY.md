# Native printing component

The native `bizhub185-filter` and `pbm-jbig` link to JBIG-KIT 2.1 by Markus Kuhn,
as carried in the archived foo2zjs source repository. Those binaries and the
sources bundled with the installer are supplied under GPL version 2 or later.
The complete accompanying license is in the installer source directory.

Source files discovered through Chrome and downloaded to the verified W volume:

- <https://github.com/koenkooi/foo2zjs/blob/master/jbig.c>
- <https://github.com/koenkooi/foo2zjs/blob/master/jbig.h>
- <https://github.com/koenkooi/foo2zjs/blob/master/jbig_ar.c>
- <https://github.com/koenkooi/foo2zjs/blob/master/jbig_ar.h>
- <https://github.com/koenkooi/foo2zjs/blob/master/COPYING>

The official Windows driver modules remain on W and are used only for local
interoperability research under the license accepted by the user. They are not
included in the new driver or its installer. Unicorn 2.1.4 and MuPDF 1.26.5 are
development verification tools; they are not used by the installed printer.
